Dear Teachers,

Before anything else, we just wanted to say thank you.

Not just for teaching us what's in the syllabus, but for explaining things again when we didn't understand, answering our endless questions, correcting our mistakes, and somehow managing all of us along the way. 🤍

A few things we know for sure... 😭
  • 📚“This is important” definitely means it's probably coming in the exam.
  • ⏰Deadlines are, in fact, very, very real.
  • 🙈Attendance somehow becomes the main character at the most unexpected moments.
  • 💻“It's a simple program” has never actually been simple.
  • 😂One tiny correction can somehow turn into an entire discussion.
  • ☕Lectures, deadlines and caffeine are basically an academic survival kit.
But honestly... 🤍

Behind all the assignments, deadlines, corrections and “did you submit it?” moments, there is something we don't say enough.

You encourage us when we're unsure, guide us when we're lost, and believe in us even when we don't completely believe in ourselves yet.

Years from now, we may not remember every lecture or every assignment, but we'll remember the teachers who made us feel capable, who pushed us to do better, and who made this journey a little more meaningful.

Thank you for being a part of our journey. We're genuinely lucky to have teachers like you. 🌷

With gratitude,

Your MCA Students 🤍
